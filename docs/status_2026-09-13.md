## Measured numbers (149M, compiled, cuDNN attention)

| seq | microbatch | tok/s | peak VRAM | MFU (PaLM) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2048 | 4 | 56.2K | 6.6 GiB | 66% |

| 2048 | 8 (eager) | 9.5K | 18.5 GiB | 11% |

Larger microbatches did not OOM: on Windows the allocator spills into host memory and runs 60x
slower. Keep peaks around 11 GiB. 8K needs microbatch 2 (to be measured before the 8K phase).

## Incidents overnight

- The first 149M benchmark silently kept running (its output was piped through grep) and shared the
  GPU with M1 for 45 minutes; M1 ran at a quarter speed and a sample-generation step stalled. Killed
  it, resumed M1 from its checkpoint (resume worked as designed), and M1 finished at full speed.
- Sample generation ran under the cuDNN-only attention restriction; cuDNN re-plans per KV length.
  All decode paths now use the efficient backend.
- Re-tokenization failed once because the portal had a shard memory-mapped (Windows file lock).
  The portal no longer caches memmaps; checkpoint writes retry on lock errors.
